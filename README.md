# HealPoint-Teleconsultation
HealPoint is a teleconsultation platform designed to make healthcare accessible to everyone, especially in underserved areas. It allows patients to consult doctors online, manage appointments, and receive digital prescriptions—all from a simple, low-bandwidth interface. Our goal is to reduce hospital load and bring healthcare to people’s fingertips.

# Problem Statement
Long waiting times in hospitals
Lack of specialists in rural/semi-urban areas
Difficulty in accessing healthcare during emergencies or pandemics
High cost of frequent hospital visits.

# HealPoint provides:
📱 Online doctor consultation (video/audio/chat)
📅 Appointment scheduling system
📄 Digital prescriptions
🔔 Notifications & reminders
🧑‍⚕️ Doctor & patient dashboards

# Tech Stack 
Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript
Backend: Firebase
Database: MySQL

# ✨ Key Features 
Real-time teleconsultation
Secure login & authentication
Role-based dashboards (Doctor/Patient)
Medical history tracking
Lightweight and accessible (works on low bandwidth)
